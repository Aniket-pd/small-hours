SMALL HOURS — A little time outside

Meet one ordinary plant
15 minutes, including your return

Look closely at one plant without picking it, naming its species or leaving a permitted path.

1. Choose a plant in view (3 min)
Use a familiar outdoor seat or spot near greenery you can already reach. Pick one plant visible from there and leave it untouched. Use a familiar spot within 3 minutes of where you start, or stay just outside your door.

2. Look for three details (9 min)
Notice an edge, a repeated shape and a color difference. Describe only what you can observe; a species name is unnecessary.

3. Create its portrait (3 min)
Make a quick mental or pencil sketch, then give it a descriptive nickname such as 'the zigzag leaf'. Keep these last 3 minutes for wrapping up and returning.

Take a little something back
A detailed portrait of one plant, left exactly where it was.

Bring: Paper and pencil (optional).
This idea needs daylight and a reachable green space.
