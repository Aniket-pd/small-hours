SMALL HOURS — A little time outside

Find your outdoor palette
15 minutes, including your return

From one familiar outdoor spot, notice a small collection of colors you would usually walk past.

1. Pick one spot (3 min)
Choose a familiar outdoor place you can use comfortably, such as your doorstep or an existing seat. Stay there for the activity. Use a familiar spot within 3 minutes of where you start, or stay just outside your door.

2. Collect colors with your eyes (9 min)
Find three different colors around you. Look for a second shade of each, without moving or collecting anything.

3. Name the palette (3 min)
Give the colors your own names and choose a title for the scene. Remember it or make a brief note. Keep these last 3 minutes for wrapping up and returning.

Take a little something back
A three-color palette named after a real moment outside.

Bring: Paper and pencil (optional).
